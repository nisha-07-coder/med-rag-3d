Who MED-RAG helps

Patient

Wants to understand information in a medical report.

Caregiver or family member

Wants to find specific information in a patient's documents.

Healthcare professional

May use it as an information-retrieval aid.

Example: "What is my current HbA1c level?"

MED-RAG helps retrieve and understand information from uploaded documents. It does not replace doctors and gives no diagnosis or treatment.

General-purpose AI vs MED-RAG

General-purpose AI assistant

  • Answers a very wide range of questions
  • Not built around one uploaded report

MED-RAG

  • Built to retrieve information from uploaded medical reports
  • Shows source filename and page for checking
  • Retrieval-based workflow
  • Can abstain when evidence is insufficient

This is a difference in design purpose, not a claim that MED-RAG is more accurate.
